Administrative Staff

The people and offices that keep the college running, as listed by the CICS Student Government.

[S1]OFFICE OF THE DEAN

Dean & Associate Dean

Dean Marivic R. Mitschek, MIT, and Associate Dean Rolando B. Barrameda, PhD, lead the college, set academic direction, and approve programs, faculty loads and college policies.

[S2]DEPARTMENTS

Department Chairs

Run the CS and IT departments: class schedules, curriculum, advising and faculty concerns. CSD: Josephine T. Eduardo, jteduardo@dlsud.edu.ph. ITD: Sherry B. Naz, itdchair@dlsud.edu.ph.

[S3]OFFICE

Secretaries

Handle student requests, forms, records and appointments. Secretary to the Dean: Rina G. Rodriguez. Secretary to the CSD Chair: Cherry P. Manzano. Secretary to the ITD Chair: Joyra Jesusa L. Joya.

[S4]LABORATORY

Runs the ICT labs, their equipment, software and reservations. Supervisor: Emelyn D. Mayuga. Technician: Homer P. Morallo. Computer Lab: local 3269.

Supports online and digital learning. Director: Roda N. Sanares, DIT. Training and Support Supervisor: Maria Gloria A. Del Rosario.

03

Research Groups & Laboratories

>>>

CICS classes run in the ICT computer laboratories, rooms ICT-201 to ICT-213. Computer Lab: local 3269.

[L1]BSCS · COMPUTER SCIENCE

ICT-201 · 203 · 205 · 207 · 209

Labs mainly used by Computer Science classes: programming, algorithms, machine learning, game development and thesis work.

[L2]BSIT · INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY

ICT-202 · 204 · 206 · 208 · 210 · 212

Labs mainly used by IT classes: web development, databases, networking, CISCO Networking courses and capstone projects.

[L3]OPEN TO ALL STUDENTS

ICT-213 · Open Lab

The open laboratory any student can use for coursework, projects and research outside class hours.

[L4]FACULTY

ICT-211 · Faculty Room

Where CICS faculty hold consultations. Drop by during their consultation hours.

[L5]RESEARCH CENTER

Center for Artificial Intelligence

The CICS center for AI work, supervised by Jennylinde R. Manaois.

[L6]INDUSTRY PARTNER · 2026

AI Innovation Lab · with WPH Digital

Set up under an agreement DLSU-D and WPH Digital signed on June 4, 2026. A proof-of-concept center where students, faculty and partners build, test and validate AI solutions with real equipment and real-world use cases.
