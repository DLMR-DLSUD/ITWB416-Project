03

Administrative Staff

>>>

The offices that keep the college running, and what each one handles. Add names when available.

[S1]OFFICE OF THE DEAN

Dean

Leads the college, sets academic direction, and approves programs, faculty loads and college policies.

[S2]DEPARTMENT

Department Chairs

Run the CS and IT departments: class schedules, curriculum, advising and faculty concerns. IT Department: itdchair@dlsud.edu.ph

[S3]PROGRAM

Practicum / OJT Coordinator

Matches students with partner companies, checks OJT requirements and tracks practicum hours.

[S4]OFFICE

College Secretary

Handles student requests, forms, records and appointments with the Dean and chairs.

[S5]LABORATORY

Laboratory Technicians

Maintain computer labs and equipment, install software and process lab reservations.

[S6]PROGRAM

CISCO Academy Instructors

Deliver the CISCO Networking courses taken by BSIT students.
