Undergraduate Programs

CICS offers two bachelor's programs, each run by its own department. BSIT holds PAASCU Level II accreditation and is a CHED Center of Development in Information Technology.

ACCESS · UNDERGRAD
IT

BS Information Technology

PAASCU Level II · CHED Center of Development

ID · BSIT-01BSIT

Program description

BSIT gives students comprehensive, hands-on training in current IT concepts and applications, from programming and databases to networks and security.

Students specialize in Web Development or Network Technology, take CISCO Networking courses, finish a two-part capstone project and complete a practicum of at least 486 hours.

Program goals

  • Apply computing concepts across different domains of IT.
  • Analyze problems and define the computing requirements for their solution.
  • Design, build, test and evaluate solutions with modern computing tools.
  • Document systems clearly for end-users and work well alone or in teams.
  • Manage system security, reliability and efficiency.
  • Act on legal, social, ethical and professional issues, and keep learning through research.

CS

BS Computer Science

Computer Science Department

ID · BSCS-02BSCS

Program description

BSCS focuses on computing theory and its applications, building abstract reasoning, analytical thinking and research skills.

Students specialize in Game Development or Intelligent Systems (artificial intelligence), write a two-part thesis and complete a practicum of at least 240 hours.

Program goals

  • Apply computing and mathematics across the domains of computer science.
  • Design, build, test and evaluate algorithmic solutions to complex problems.
  • Document computer-based systems with clear, effective reports.
  • Communicate well with computing and non-computing professionals.
  • Act on legal, social, ethical and professional issues in technology.
  • Keep growing professionally through independent or team research.

03

Who Can Apply

>>>

Both programs accept three kinds of applicants. Admission and placement are based on the DLSU-D Student Admissions Test (DSAT) and your high school records; see the official undergraduate admission page for schedules and fees.

[A1]FRESHMEN

First-year students

Senior High School completers, equivalent secondary completers from abroad, and ALS passers certified for college.

[A2]TRANSFEREES

Transferring students

Students who completed no more than 75% of a curriculum at another CHED-recognized or foreign college.

[A3]SECOND COURSE

Degree holders

Graduates of any college degree from a CHED-recognized or foreign institution.

Specializations & Tracks

>>>

Each program offers two specializations, each with its own set of courses in the upper years.

[T1]BSIT · SPECIALIZATION

Web Development

Web systems and technologies, integrative programming, human-computer interaction and databases.

[T2]BSIT · SPECIALIZATION

Network Technology

Networking, CISCO Networking Academy courses, IoT and systems administration.

[T3]BSCS · SPECIALIZATION

Game Development

Introduction to Game Programming, Game Design Principles & Practice, Computer Animation & Simulation, Immersive Game Development, Advanced Game Programming, 3D Graphics & Animation.
