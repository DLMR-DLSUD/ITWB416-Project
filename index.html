About the College

>>>

CICS is DLSU-D's home for technology education, built on academic excellence, innovation and strong ethics.

Students, faculty and industry partners work together on real technological problems. Through its Computer Science and Information Technology departments, the college prepares future leaders who make meaningful contributions to the digital world.
