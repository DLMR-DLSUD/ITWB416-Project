About the College

>>>

CICS is DLSU-D's home for technology education, built on academic excellence, innovation and strong ethics.

Students, faculty and industry partners work together on real technological problems. Through its Computer Science and Information Technology departments, the college prepares future leaders who make meaningful contributions to the digital world.

[E1]ESPORTS · NOV 4–9, 2026

Chasing Victory: Game On!

CICSSG's gaming convention and esports tournament during Students' Week. Valorant and Mobile Legends: Bang Bang eliminations ran Nov 4–7. The main event on Nov 9 (10:00 AM–5:00 PM) filled the PBH Courtyard, Salrial Hall and Tanghalang Julian Felipe with championships, cosplay judging and student booths.

[E2]CAREERS · JUNE 2026

Prospace 2026

CICSSG's tech and career expo: online career sessions on June 6, then job fairs on June 9–10 at Salrial Hall and the PBH Courtyard, open to all La Salle schools. prospace.cicssg.com

[E3]SPORTS · NOV 2026

AthleCICS 2026

The CICS interdepartmental sportsfest, with basketball, volleyball, badminton and table tennis. The CS and IT program councils announced this year's edition for the week of Nov 4–8.

Capstone & Thesis Turnover

Through the BIGKIS extension project, CICS turned over 24 student-built web systems and mobile apps to partner communities and offices.

The national CS and IT skills competition hosted by CICSSG for senior high and college students: skills contests, a hackathon, oral research presentations and quiz bees.
