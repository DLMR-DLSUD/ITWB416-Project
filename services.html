05

Laboratory Policies

>>>

Labs are shared by everyone. Keep them working. Sample rules. Replace with the official lab policy.

// rules.txt

Computer Lab Rules

  • Enter only with your class or an approved reservation, and show your student ID.
  • No food or drinks near the computers.
  • Do not install software, change settings or unplug equipment.
  • Save your files to your own storage. Lab PCs are wiped regularly.
  • Report broken equipment to the lab technician right away.
  • Log out and leave your station clean before you go.

// booking.sh

Booking a Lab

  1. Fill out the Lab Reservation Form (see Forms below).
  2. Have it signed by your instructor or org adviser.
  3. Submit it to the lab technician at least 3 working days ahead.
  4. Wait for confirmation before using the room.
