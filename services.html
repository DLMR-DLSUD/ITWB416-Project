Enrollment & Registration

>>>

The usual steps each semester, following the Office of the University Registrar. Second semester, A.Y. 2026–2027: online enrollment for all college levels runs January 5–15, 2027, and classes open on January 18, 2027.

  1. 01

    Check advisories

    Watch for the enlistment schedule for your year level and program.

  2. 02

    Consult your adviser

    Confirm your subjects, prerequisites and load for the term.

  3. 03

    Enlist online

    Choose your sections through the student portal within your schedule.

  4. 04

    Pay assessment

    Settle your fees or arrange your payment plan or scholarship.

  5. 05

    Get validated

    Download your registration form and keep a copy for the semester.

Good to know

  • Adding / dropping: use New Transaction in Online Enrollment, up to the third transaction.
  • Refunds on withdrawal: 25% in week 1, 50% in week 2, none after that.
  • Petition classes: filed in the Student Portal and approved by the Program Chair once enough students sign up.
  • Tutorial classes: graduating students only, at most 2.
  • Leave of absence: filed at the College Dean's Office, up to 1 year, at most 2 times.
  • Retention: failing 24 units makes you ineligible to enroll the next semester.

Registrar: Ayuntamiento de Gonzalez Bldg., Mon–Fri 8:00 AM–12:00 NN & 1:00–4:00 PM · oursecretary@dlsud.edu.ph · local 3025

Laboratory Policies

>>>

CICS uses the ICT labs, rooms ICT-201 to ICT-213. Odd-numbered rooms (201–209) are mainly for CS classes and even-numbered rooms (202–212) for IT classes. ICT-213 is the open lab for any student, and ICT-211 is the faculty room.

// rules.txt

Computer Lab Rules

  • Use the lab only during your class schedule or with an approved reservation. ICT-213 is open to any student outside class hours.
  • Keep food and drinks away from the computers.
  • Don't install software, change settings, or unplug or move equipment.
  • Use the computers for schoolwork only. Save games and streaming for later.
  • Report broken or missing equipment to the lab technician right away.

// booking.sh

Booking a Lab

  1. Fill out the Lab Reservation Form (see Forms below).
  2. Have it signed by your instructor or org adviser.
  3. Submit it to the lab technician at least 3 working days ahead. CICS Computer Lab: local 3269.
  4. Wait for confirmation before using the room.

Scholarships & Financial Assistance

>>>

Scholarship programs offered through the DLSU-D Admissions and Scholarship Office. Apply through the Scholarship Unit: scholarship@dlsud.edu.ph · local 3029. Full details are on the official scholarship page.

[F1]MERIT · FRESHMEN

Entrance Scholarship

For Rank 1 and Rank 2 graduates of a batch of at least 100 from a DepEd-recognized school: 100% and 50% off tuition for one semester.

[F2]MERIT · UPPERCLASS

Academic Scholarship

For the top 30% by semester GPA, with no grade below 3.25 and at least 18 units. Discounts range from 10% to 100% depending on rank.

[F3]NEED

Student Financial Aid Grant

Tuition discounts for students who need support. The Scholarship Committee sets the amount.

[F4]TALENT

Athletic & Performing Arts

For varsity players and members of the university's performing arts groups, endorsed by their coach or group.

[F5]SERVICE

ROTC & Vicissitude

Tuition discounts for 267th NROTC officers and for members of the Vicissitude yearbook staff.

[F6]EXTERNAL · KALINGA

Sponsored Grants

The Lasallian SPOON, the One Dasma Dormitory Scholarship Grant and Alumni Legacy Night grants.
