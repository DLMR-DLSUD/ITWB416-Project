College Profile

>>>

The College of Information and Computer Studies (CICS) is the computing college of De La Salle University-Dasmariñas, committed to academic excellence, innovation and ethical standards.

The college brings students, faculty and industry partners together in an inclusive environment to work on complex technological problems. It runs two departments, Computer Science and Information Technology, and offers the BS Computer Science and BS Information Technology programs.

CICS aims to prepare future leaders who excel in their professions and contribute meaningfully to the global digital landscape.

History

>>>

A changelog of the university CICS grew up in. Add the year CICS itself was established once confirmed.

  1. v1.0 · 1977

    General Emilio Aguinaldo College-Cavite

    The institution opens in Dasmariñas, Cavite.

  2. v2.0 · 1987

    Joins the La Salle family

    Becomes De La Salle University-Emilio Aguinaldo College.

  3. v3.0 · 1992

    DLSU-Aguinaldo

    The university is renamed De La Salle University-Aguinaldo.

  4. v4.0 · 1997

    DLSU-Dasmariñas

    Takes its current name, De La Salle University-Dasmariñas.

  5. v5.0 · 2026

    Level III accreditation

    CICS programs earn Level III status, valid until 2030.
