College Profile

>>>

The College of Information and Computer Studies (CICS) is the computing college of De La Salle University-Dasmariñas, committed to academic excellence, innovation and ethical standards.

Established in 2024 when the College of Science and Computer Studies split in two, the college brings students, faculty and industry partners together in an inclusive environment to work on complex technological problems. It runs two departments, Computer Science and Information Technology, and offers the BS Computer Science and BS Information Technology programs.

CICS aims to prepare future leaders who excel in their professions and contribute meaningfully to the global digital landscape.

03

History

>>>

A changelog of CICS, from the university's founding to the college's own launch in 2024.

  1. v1.0 · 1977

    Institutional roots

    Founded on July 18, 1977, as General Emilio Aguinaldo College-Cavite in Dasmariñas, Cavite.

  2. v2.0 · 1987

    Joins the La Salle family

    De La Salle University acquires EAC-Cavite, which becomes De La Salle University-Emilio Aguinaldo College under Br. Andrew Gonzalez FSC. It is renamed DLSU-Aguinaldo in 1992.

  3. v3.0 · 1996

    DLSU-Dasmariñas & campus IT

    The university takes its current name. A systems development group is formed to support campus IT, and the Information and Communications Technology Center (ICTC) is later built to house modern computer labs.

  4. v4.0 · 2004

    Center of Development in IT

    DLSU-D receives its own university charter from CHED, and its computing unit is named a CHED Center of Development in Information Technology.

  5. v5.0 · pre-2024

    The CSCS era

    BSCS and BSIT are offered by the Computer Studies Department of the College of Science and Computer Studies (CSCS) at Paulo Campos Hall. BSIT earns PAASCU Level II accreditation.

  6. v6.0 · Aug–Sep 2024

    CICS is established

    CSCS splits into the College of Science and the College of Information and Computer Studies. The Computer Studies Department becomes two departments, Computer Science and Information Technology, and the new CICS Student Government (CICSSG) takes over from the CSCS Student Government.

  7. v7.0 · today

    Growing partnerships

    CICS builds industry and international links, including an AI Innovation Lab with WPH Digital (2026) and the Java Journey Immersion in Indonesia for BSIT students.

04

The Dean

>>>

Marivic R. Mitschek is the Dean of the College of Information and Computer Studies. She has held a dean's post since 2019 and has led CICS since the college was formed in 2024. Before that, she chaired the Computer Studies Department, and she remains part of the full-time IT faculty.

She holds a BS in Computer Science and a Master in Information Technology. Under her leadership, CICS turned over 24 student-built capstone and thesis systems to partner communities through its BIGKIS extension project in April 2026. She stressed that the project links academic innovation with community empowerment. In June 2026 she joined the signing that set up an AI Innovation Lab with industry partner WPH Digital. Her research includes Kwentong Pambata, an interactive storybook of Filipino fables, legends and parables.
